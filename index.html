OUR CAMPUS STORY

Made for Campus Life.

BiteBurp started in a regular campus hostel room out of pure frustration. Every evening around 6:30 PM after working out or grinding through endless assignment deadlines, our only food choices were either oily canteen fried food or overpriced protein bars from the convenience store.

We asked ourselves: Why can't we just have a fresh, filling, healthy snack box made with everyday natural ingredients, priced affordably for a college pocket?

So we bought fresh oats, honest peanut butter, ripe bananas, and seeds, packed them neatly, and gave them to our friends in the hostel. They loved it. That was day one of BiteBurp.

🌱 Zero Nonsense

No chemical powders or misleading fitness buzzwords. Just honest fuel.

🤝 Student-Powered

Prepared, packed and managed entirely by students right here on campus.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering and picking up BiteBurp.

You can place your order anytime before 6:00 PM for the evening batch. Order distribution and pickups happen daily between 6:00 PM and 8:00 PM at our campus pickup spots.

During checkout, you can select your preferred pickup point: Campus Gym Entrance, Hostel Block C Lawn, SAC Portico, or Library Back Stand. A BiteBurp volunteer with a cooler box will be waiting there with your token ready.

We use simple direct UPI payments. When placing an order, scan the UPI QR code using Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM, make the payment of ₹59, and submit the 12-digit transaction/UTR reference ID to receive your pickup token.

Your token (e.g., #BB-104) is your digital claim ticket. Just show it on your phone or say your token number when you arrive at the pickup spot to grab your box instantly without waiting in queues.

Yes! You can increase the quantity in the order form (up to 10 boxes per order) for your gym buddy or roommate. The total amount updates automatically.

Because we prepare everything fresh each day to avoid food waste, we operate with a limited daily batch (usually 25–50 boxes). If stock runs out, the status bar will show "SOLD OUT" and orders will open again the next morning.
